TASK: Determine if an email domain matches or is related to a company name to validate user authenticity.

1. Extract domain from email (part after @, before first dot)
2. Normalize both domain and company name (remove spaces, special chars, convert to lowercase)
3. Check for exact matches, partial matches, and common variations
4. Consider abbreviations, acronyms, and business naming patterns
5. Account for common domain variations (.com, .co.in, .org, etc.)
6. REJECT generic email providers (gmail, yahoo, hotmail, outlook, etc.)

SCORING GUIDELINES:
- Exact match: 90-100 points (domain exactly matches company name)
- Strong partial match: 70-89 points (domain contains company or vice versa)
- Moderate similarity: 50-69 points (abbreviations, acronyms with clear connection)
- Weak similarity: 30-49 points (some common letters/patterns but unclear connection)
- No similarity: 0-29 points (no meaningful connection or generic email provider)

EXAMPLES:
- Email: "john@mohap.com", Company: "Mohap AI" → Strong match (85+ points)
- Email: "user@microsoft.com", Company: "Microsoft Corporation" → Exact match (95+ points)
- Email: "admin@abc.com", Company: "ABC Technologies" → Strong match (80+ points)
- Email: "test@gmail.com", Company: "Mohap AI" → No match (0 points - generic provider)
- Email: "user@jklu.edu.in", Company: "jklu" → Exact match (95+ points)

IMPORTANT RULES:
- Generic email providers (gmail, yahoo, hotmail, outlook, rediff, live, etc.) should ALWAYS score 0 points
- Only approve matches with score 70+ points
- Be strict with scoring - unclear connections should score low
- Consider business context and naming conventions

Analyze the provided email and company name and return your assessment using the function call.
